MetalForecasting Architecture & Methodology

Complete breakdown of the macroeconomic data pipeline, regime switching engine, and Monte Carlo simulation parameters.

1. Data Pipeline & Sourcing

Macro series are pulled directly from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), including FEDFUNDS (Fed Rate), CPIAUCSL (Consumer Price Index), DGS10 (10-Year Treasury Yield), DGS2 (2-Year Treasury Yield), DTWEXBGS (Trade Weighted U.S. Dollar Index), VIXCLS (CBOE Volatility Index), and STLFSI4 (St. Louis Financial Stress Index). Commodity price anchors are synced via Yahoo Finance (GC=F for Gold, SI=F for Silver, USDINR=X for Rupee).

2. Feature Engineering & Regimes

Key financial features include 12-month CPI Inflation, 10Y-2Y Treasury Spread, and Real Sovereign Yields (10Y minus Inflation). A 2-component Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) partitions historical epochs into distinct market regimes. The regime with the higher average VIX is tagged as the Crisis Regime.

3. Gradient Boosting Ensembles

Independent GradientBoostingRegressor models are fitted for Gold and Silver under both Normal and Crisis regimes. In each Monte Carlo scenario, the model predicts the monthly return conditioned on simulated macro conditions.

4. Percentile Confidence Intervals

The 10th percentile (P10) represents conservative support (bearish scenario), the Mean represents the expected base case, and the 90th percentile (P90) denotes the bullish resistance target.
